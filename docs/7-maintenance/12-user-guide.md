# 12 · User Guide

Open https://sejuk-sejuk-roan.vercel.app and pick a demo account on the sign-in page (one click). Times are Malaysia time; money is RM.

## Admin (Aisyah)

1. **New order** (top bar): customer, phone (any Malaysian format), address, problem, service type, quote, date and time. Pick a technician: the list shows their jobs that day and warns about clashes within two hours. Submit to get the order number; tap **Notify on WhatsApp** to message the technician.
2. **Orders**: search by order number, name or phone; filter by status, technician, dates or "has flags". Open an order to edit, reassign or reschedule (a reason is asked for once it is assigned) and to see the full history.
3. **Flags**: raise a flag on any order (High / Medium / Low with a note); resolve with a note from the order or the **Flags** page.
4. **Customer feedback**: on a finished order, tap **Send feedback request on WhatsApp** to (re)send it.

## Technician (Ali, John, Bala, Yusoff — use a phone)

1. **Today** shows your jobs (and any still open from earlier). Tap a job → **Navigate** or **Call**.
2. **Start job**, then fill **Complete job**: work done, extra charges (the final amount works itself out), remarks, photos/videos/PDF (up to 6), payment and receipt if collected. **Mark job done** records your name and the time.
3. Send the customer the feedback request on WhatsApp from the success screen.
4. Flags the office raised on your job appear on the job page.

## Manager (Rahman)

1. **Home**: today's tiles, open flags, the technician × hour schedule (red line = now) and the review queue. It updates by itself.
2. **Review**: approve, send back (say what to redo) or close reviewed orders (a note is required if payment is short).
3. **Dashboard**: this week / last week / this month / custom dates; cards, leaderboard, charts (each with **Show as table**).
4. **Calendar**: list / day / week / month (phones open on the list), filter by technician, click a job to open it.
5. **Team**: deactivate or reactivate technicians (reassign their open jobs first). **Audit**: every change, filterable. **AI log**: questions the assistant refused.

## Everyone

- **Bell** (admin, manager): live alerts — High flags individually, other completions grouped. **Sound on/off** sits next to it (technicians: top right).
- **AI button** (round, bottom right): ask about jobs, technicians, schedules or flags, e.g. "Who might be overloaded next week?". Answers name their source. You see only what your role may see; it refuses anything else, and it can only read, never change data. 20 questions an hour.
